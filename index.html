FAQs

Everything You Need to Know

Everything you need to know about creating Amazon listing creative with Listing Optimization AI.

What exactly does this tool do?

It turns an Amazon product link into customer research, competitive insights, listing images, infographics, and A+ content in one connected workflow.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. You can start a free trial without a credit card and explore the core creative workflow before choosing a plan.

Do I need any design or AI experience?

No. The guided workflow handles the research, layout, and generation steps. You provide the product and approve or refine the output.

Can the tool replace a designer?

It can handle a large share of routine listing production and iteration. Many teams still use designers for art direction, final brand review, and complex launches.

Can I generate full image sets, not just one image?

Yes. You can create main images, benefit infographics, lifestyle concepts, variant visuals, and complete A+ content modules.

How long does it take to generate everything?

Most first drafts are generated in minutes. Final timing depends on the number of assets, revisions, and brand approvals in your workflow.

Will this actually help my CTR and conversion rate?

The system is designed around proven Amazon conversion frameworks and customer insights. Results vary by product, category, traffic, offer, and execution, so we recommend validating improvements with tests.

Will it work for my product category?

It supports a broad range of Amazon categories by analyzing the product, reviews, competitors, and category context before generating creative.

Do the images generated follow Amazon's rules?

The workflow is designed with Amazon listing conventions in mind, but requirements can change by category and marketplace. Always review final assets against the current Seller Central guidelines before publishing.

Can it analyze my Amazon reviews automatically?

The current creative studio does not fetch review data automatically. Bring verified findings and approved customer language into the product brief so they can guide the creative without becoming unsubstantiated claims.

How accurate are the competitive insights?

Insights are grounded in the listing and review data available during analysis. Treat them as decision support and verify any business-critical claim against its source.

Does this replace split testing?

No. It helps you produce stronger hypotheses and variants faster; split testing remains the best way to confirm what wins with your actual shoppers.

Is my data private?

Workspace data is used to provide the service and should be handled according to the platform's privacy and security terms. Contact the team for enterprise data requirements.

Can I collaborate with my team inside the tool?

Yes. Team workflows support shared brands, feedback, and coordinated creative reviews, with expanded controls available for agencies and enterprises.

Do you offer support if I get stuck?

Yes. Support is included across plans, with priority support and a dedicated account manager available for qualifying plans.
